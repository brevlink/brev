# Product scope

## Deliberately out of scope in this phase

- **Link expiration:** useful, but the current link model has no expiration date; the behavior of an expired link (404, a dedicated page, or deletion) must be decided before implementation.
- **Password-protected links:** require an intermediate page and a visitor session, changing the simple redirect model and click statistics; they must be designed together with those statistics.
- **Tags and folders:** useful once users have many links, rather than before; they must be designed together with search.
